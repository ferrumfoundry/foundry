How a mission runs · start to shipped

Three steps.
That's the job.

  1. 01

    Brief the team

    Tell Echo what to study. Echo writes the mission brief, splits the work — scouting, architecture, docs, code — and hands each bot its part.

  2. 02

    Watch the work

    Each bot works on its own screen. The chat shows every step as it happens, finished work folds into a summary, and you can open any screen to watch — or take over.

  3. 03

    Approve, then it ships

    Anything that writes to the outside world waits for your OK on an approval card. Anvil builds the clean-room version, Forge reviews it, Echo reports back.

Before you ask

Frequently asked.

What is Foundry?

Foundry is a team of five AI bots — Echo, Ember, Forge, Ledger and Anvil — working together on one computer. Each bot has its own screen, email, phone number and memory. You brief them in chat, watch them work, and approve anything that leaves the machine. Their specialty is studying a product and building Ferrum's own clean-room version of it.

What do I get when I download?

The v0.4 preview build: the Foundry team interface with a complete sample mission, for Windows (a ZIP — unzip and double-click Start-Foundry) or Android (an APK that installs like any app). It runs offline and needs no account. The bots connect live in the full release.

Is Foundry finished?

Not yet — it's in active development, and the preview is the honest current build: the finished interface and workflow, with the live bot connection as the next stage. The design — the five bots, their roles, the phone and desktop layouts — is final.

Why does the team study other products?

To learn how they're built — then Ferrum builds its own version from that understanding. Original code and original assets, every time. Nothing is copied from the product being studied.

Who makes Foundry?

Foundry is a Ferrum project, designed and built by Jakob Howarth. Echo — the host bot — is also Jakob's design, and leads the team from inside the app.
