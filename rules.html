Foundry Rules

Acceptable use · Effective November 15, 2026

These Rules are a plain-English draft for the Foundry prototype, reviewed before launch.

What Foundry is for

Foundry is Ferrum's multi-agent team app. It is for gaming, building apps, listings, and other honest business work. The teammates (Echo, Ember, Forge, Ledger and Anvil) will only help with work that fits these Rules.

The bots follow these Rules at all times

Every message and mission brief is checked before it reaches a bot. If a request breaks these Rules, Foundry will refuse it. A serious violation, or three blocked requests, will ban the account. If your account is banned, you can send a help ticket and ask us to review it.

What you must not do

You may not use Foundry to ask for, plan, or carry out any of the following:

  • Hacking or breaking into websites, accounts, programs, networks or other systems you do not have permission to use.
  • Making or spreading malware, exploits, viruses or other harmful software.
  • Fraud, scams, phishing, or stealing money, credentials or personal data.
  • Harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, or abusive attacks on other people.
  • Illegal content or help with criminal activity.
  • Sexual content involving minors (anyone under 18). This is strictly prohibited.
  • Spam, mass unsolicited messages, or other abusive bulk activity.
  • Evading usage limits, safety filters, a ban, or other protections in the app.

The team computer

The shared team computer is for the same kind of work. Do not use it for scanners, attack tools, malware, or mass unsolicited requests.

If you break the Rules

Foundry may refuse a request, pause the account, or ban it. There is no extra charge for a safety pause or a ban. If you think a decision was made in error, please make a help ticket from the ban screen or from Settings and we will review it.

Reports, help tickets and ban appeals go to Foundry's official account, where our moderation team reviews them.

Changes

We may update these Rules. When we do, we will change the effective date and may ask you to agree again before you keep using Foundry.
