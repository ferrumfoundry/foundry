Privacy Policy

How Ferrum handles information in Foundry · Effective November 15, 2026

This policy is a draft for the product prototype and should be reviewed before launch.

Who we are

This policy describes how Ferrum ("we") handles information when you use Foundry. It is a draft for the product prototype and should be reviewed before launch.

What we collect

Depending on how you use Foundry, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address and account id, and the plan or tokens on the account.
  • Messages, mission briefs, files and other content you send to the team, plus the team's replies and work product.
  • Usage information, such as how often you message or start a mission, computer-time use, safety checks, and basic device or log data needed to run the app.
  • Help tickets you submit, including your email and what you tell us about a problem or an appeal.

Why we collect it

We use this information to operate Foundry, sign you in, run the team, apply the plan and usage limits you are on, enforce the Rules, review help tickets, fix problems, and improve the product. We do not use your content to sell advertising.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We may use service providers who help us host or operate Foundry, and we may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people from serious harm.

Retention

We keep account, message and usage data while your account is open and for a reasonable period after, so we can operate the service, resolve disputes and meet legal duties. Safety and ban records may be kept longer if needed to protect the service. In this prototype, much of that data is stored only on your device.

Deletion and other requests

You can ask us to delete your account or the personal information we hold, or to correct it, by sending a help ticket in the app. We will review the request and explain if we need to keep a limited record (for example, a ban or a legal obligation).

Minors

Foundry is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. Sexual content involving anyone under 18 is banned.

Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or other help, please make a help ticket in Foundry (Settings, then Help ticket). A public support email will be added here when Foundry is published.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will change the effective date and, if the changes are material, we may ask you to agree again.
